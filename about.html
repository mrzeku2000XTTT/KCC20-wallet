KCC20 Wallet

KCC20 Wallet is the non-custodial Kaspa wallet for native KAS, KCC20 covenant tokens (KRON, KKDAG / Kas Knight, and others), KRC-20 (Kasplex), time-lock vaults, and Scorpion. Open it in any browser. Keys never leave your device.

Open KCC20 Wallet Developer SDK

What you can do

  • Send and receive Kaspa (KAS) on mainnet and TN10.
  • Hold and trade KCC20 tokens with the KRON SDK — wallet-built, never a hosted node hop.
  • Lock KAS in covenant vaults (CLTV). Sweep when the timer ends.
  • Connect dApps with window.kcc20 — no Chrome extension.
  • Open TTT, KasDistro, KBUILD, and K Social from You → Apps.
