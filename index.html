              How this bridge works
              
    
                
  • Not P2P. You are not matched to another person. You trade against KRON’s on-chain curve or locked AMM pool — a covenant on Kaspa L1.
    • 
                
  • Not a chain wrap. This is not Ethereum↔Kaspa. Both tokens are already KCC20. The only hop is KAS, because KRON has no token↔token pool.
    • 
                
  • Two legs, two signatures. 1) Sell FROM for KAS. 2) Buy TO with that KAS. Keys stay in this wallet. We never hold the funds.
    • 
                
  • Curve vs pool. New tokens trade on a bonding curve. After graduation they trade on a locked pool. The quote names which venue each token uses.
    • 
                
  • Oracle. Quotes are live KRON indexer reads of the curve/pool reserves (idx.kron.technology), not a made-up price. After you sell KKDAG, the app waits for the KAS UTXO, then re-quotes IFWEN (or TO) from fresh reserves and buys. Then it checks your TO cells so you actually received it.
    • 
                
  • Example. KKDAG → IFWEN: sell KKDAG for KAS on its venue, then buy IFWEN with that KAS on IFWEN’s venue.

            How DCA works (read this)
            
    
              
  1. Pick any KCC20 — KRON, KROSHI, KKDAG, or another ticker on KRON.
    2. 
              
  2. First buy happens when you sign — that slice goes into the KRON market immediately.
    3.

    4. 
              
  4. Then wait your interval — budget ÷ each buy = number of slices. Timer × remaining slices = how long the plan runs.
    5. 
            

            
If you close the tab, capsules wait on-chain until you open the app again (even locked we can broadcast the drip). Token buys run when this wallet is unlocked. You can stop and reclaim unopened capsules.
